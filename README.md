## 🧠 Intelligence Engine · Mesin Kecerdasan

<table>
<tr>
<td width="50%">

### 🇬🇧 Four Intelligence Systems

**🔗 CONTEXTUAL INTELLIGENCE**
Understands relationships between appointments, tasks, commitments, and logistics. Never just lists items — evaluates feasibility, sequence, and impact.

**🔮 ANTICIPATORY INTELLIGENCE**
Sees what's coming before you need to ask. Flags upcoming conflicts, preparation requirements, and hidden dependencies automatically.

**⚖️ DECISION INTELLIGENCE**
When you face a choice, it structures the decision: options → advantages → risks → recommendation → reasoning. Clarity, not overwhelm.

**⚙️ EXECUTION INTELLIGENCE**
Moves beyond recommendations into actual deliverables: drafted emails, completed schedules, organized checklists, structured plans, research summaries.

</td>
<td width="50%">

### 🇮🇩 Empat Sistem Kecerdasan

**🔗 KECERDASAN KONTEKSTUAL**
Memahami hubungan antar janji, tugas, komitmen, dan logistik. Tidak hanya mendaftar item — mengevaluasi kelayakan, urutan, dan dampak.

**🔮 KECERDASAN ANTISIPATIF**
Melihat apa yang akan datang sebelum kamu perlu bertanya. Menandai konflik mendatang, persyaratan persiapan, dan dependensi tersembunyi secara otomatis.

**⚖️ KECERDASAN KEPUTUSAN**
Ketika kamu menghadapi pilihan, ia menstrukturkan keputusan: opsi → keuntungan → risiko → rekomendasi → alasan. Kejernihan, bukan kebingungan.

**⚙️ KECERDASAN EKSEKUSI**
Melampaui rekomendasi ke hasil nyata: email yang sudah disusun, jadwal yang sudah lengkap, checklist yang terorganisir, rencana terstruktur, ringkasan riset.

</td>
</tr>
</table>

### 🇬🇧 Trust Architecture

Personal conversations, family information, financial data, and private documents are always treated as strictly private.

Drafting ≠ Sending. Research ≠ Purchasing. Preparing ≠ Executing. Every consequential action requires your explicit approval.

**NO FAKE CONFIRMATIONS**
If a reservation is not confirmed, it will not be claimed as confirmed. If a message was only drafted, it will not be reported as sent.

**EXTERNAL CONTENT SAFETY**
